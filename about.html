About Meenat Hospital

Registered in 2012. Rooted in Jabi ever since.

Meenat Hospital was formally registered as a healthcare provider in Abuja and has operated continuously from the same address in Jabi — built around the everyday needs of the district it serves.

Mission

To give Jabi and the surrounding Abuja area dependable, registered healthcare — from routine consultations to emergency response — without patients needing to travel across the city for basic services.

Vision

To be the hospital a family in this district turns to first, at every stage of life, because the standard of care has stayed consistent since 2012.

Approach

Departments share one clinical record and one governance structure, so a case that moves from outpatient to laboratory to pharmacy stays coherent from start to finish.

Formal Standing

A matter of public record.

Meenat Hospital operates as a registered healthcare facility. Our registration details are published here as a matter of transparency, and are available on request at the front desk.

We treat this registration as a baseline, not a marketing point — it is the minimum standard we hold every department to, year over year.

Our History

How the hospital has grown from one registration.

12 October 2012

Meenat Hospital is formally registered

The hospital is registered under RC 1071190 and opens its doors at Plot 741, Cadastral Zone B4, Jabi.

Following years

Outpatient and laboratory services expand

General outpatient care is joined by an on-site laboratory, reducing the need for patients to seek diagnostics elsewhere.

Ongoing

Maternity, paediatrics and emergency care added

The hospital builds out dedicated departments to cover the fuller range of care a growing district needs.

Today

A full-service facility in Jabi

Meenat Hospital now runs eight core departments from the same registered address, with a 24-hour emergency desk.
