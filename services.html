Our Departments

Eight departments. One registered facility.

Every service below is delivered from our Jabi address, so care that crosses departments — a lab test ordered from outpatient, a prescription filled after a consultation — happens without sending you elsewhere.

Department 01

Emergency & Trauma

Our emergency desk and resuscitation bay are staffed around the clock, every day of the year. Cases are triaged on arrival and escalated to the appropriate department immediately — outpatient, maternity or surgical referral.

Includes: triage, resuscitation, stabilisation, ambulance coordination.

Department 02

Maternity & Gynaecology

Antenatal appointments, delivery suites and postnatal follow-up are handled by the same team throughout, so a patient's history carries from first booking through to discharge.

Includes: antenatal care, delivery, postnatal checks, gynaecological consultations.

Department 03

Paediatrics

Growth monitoring, routine immunisation and treatment for childhood illness, in rooms and waiting areas set up for younger patients.

Includes: immunisation, growth checks, acute paediatric care.

Department 04

Laboratory & Diagnostics

An on-site lab handles routine blood work and testing ordered from any department, with results returned same-day in most cases so treatment isn't delayed.

Includes: blood tests, screening panels, imaging referrals.

More Departments

Rounding out everyday care.

General Outpatient

Walk-in consultations for everyday illness, referrals and follow-up visits.

Physiotherapy

Structured rehabilitation programmes after injury or surgery.

Dental Care

Routine cleaning, extractions and restorative dental treatment.

Pharmacy

On-site dispensing so prescriptions are ready before you leave.
