Our Departments

Eight psychiatric departments. One registered facility.

Every service below is delivered from our Jabi address, so care that crosses departments — a lab test ordered from outpatient psychiatry, a prescription filled after a consultation — happens without sending you elsewhere.

Department 01

Psychiatric Emergency & Crisis Care

Our crisis desk and stabilisation bay are staffed around the clock, every day of the year. Cases are triaged on arrival and escalated to the appropriate department immediately — outpatient psychiatry, inpatient admission or specialist referral.

Includes: crisis triage, stabilisation, risk assessment, inpatient admission coordination.

Department 02

Adult Psychiatry

Diagnostic assessments, medication management and ongoing review are handled by the same team throughout, so a patient's history carries from first consultation through to discharge.

Includes: diagnostic assessment, medication management, mood and anxiety disorders, ongoing review.

Department 03

Child & Adolescent Psychiatry

Developmental and behavioural assessments, therapy and family-inclusive treatment planning, in rooms and waiting areas set up for younger patients.

Includes: developmental assessment, behavioural therapy, family counselling, school-related support.

Department 04

Laboratory & Diagnostics

An on-site lab handles routine blood work and medication-monitoring tests ordered from any department, with results returned same-day in most cases so treatment isn't delayed.

Includes: blood tests, medication-level monitoring, screening panels, referrals for imaging.

More Departments

Rounding out everyday mental health care.

Psychiatric Outpatient Clinic

Walk-in consultations, referrals and routine follow-up visits.

Psychological Counselling & Psychotherapy

Structured individual and group therapy programmes.

Substance Use & Addiction Treatment

Supervised detox, rehabilitation and relapse-prevention support.

Pharmacy

On-site dispensing so psychiatric prescriptions are ready before you leave.
