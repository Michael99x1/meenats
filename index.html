About Meenat Hospital

A neighbourhood hospital, held to a formal standard.

Meenat Hospital was established and formally registered in 2012, and has operated from Jabi ever since. We built our services around what a growing district in Abuja actually needs day to day: a dependable outpatient clinic, a maternity ward, a laboratory that turns results around quickly, and an emergency desk that never closes.

Every department here reports into the same clinical governance structure, so a referral from one unit to another — say, from outpatient to laboratory to pharmacy — happens without a patient having to repeat themselves at each stop.
